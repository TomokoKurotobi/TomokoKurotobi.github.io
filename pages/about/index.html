A little introduction

I cook, code, troubleshoot, and collect new curiosities.

I made this website as a place for the parts of me that do not fit neatly inside a professional profile. It is where a cooking experiment can sit beside a tiny browser game, and where unfinished ideas are welcome.

I like cooking because it combines careful technique with room to experiment. Trying different flavors, arranging a plate, and turning a collection of ingredients into something memorable feels surprisingly similar to programming.

In the kitchenI enjoy French technique, Indian flavors, beautiful plating, and a very sharp knife.
At the keyboardI like puzzles, performance problems, visual tools, and making complicated systems understandable.
AlwaysCurious, patient, detail-oriented, and ready to help someone untangle a stubborn problem.
